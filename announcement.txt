🇭🇷 Croatian

Pozdrav svima!

Ljeto znači da je vrijeme za još jedan Zagreb Developers Drinkup! 🍻

U petak, 28.8., ponovno se okuplja nekoliko zagrebačkih developerskih meetup grupa na zajedničkom druženju uz cugu i dobru ekipu. Popis grupa koje sudjeluju: https://zagreb-developers.github.io/drinkup

Ove godine družimo se u Zmajskoj Pivovari "SIP", Trnjanska cesta 111a (https://maps.app.goo.gl/zHcCyEyGsi5VBwPV9), od 18h do ponoći.

Format ostaje isti: nema pripremljenih predavanja, prezentacija ni posebnog programa — samo piće, casual razgovori i prilika da upoznate druge developere iz zagrebačke tech zajednice.

Budući da se više meetup grupa okuplja zajedno, ovo je odlična prilika da izađete iz svoje uobičajene meetup ekipe, upoznate nove ljude i vidite čime se bave drugi developeri i zajednice. A s obzirom na to da očekujemo više ljudi nego na našim redovnim drinkupima, vjerujemo da će atmosfera biti posebno vesela!

Slobodno pozovite prijatelje, kolege ili druge ljude iz tech zajednice, čak i ako nisu aktivni članovi nekog od meetupova. Ako ste developer — pozvani ste!

Vidimo se! 🍻

🇬🇧 English

Hi everyone!

Summer means it's time for another Zagreb Developers Drinkup! 🍻

On Friday, August 28th, several local developer meetup groups will once again get together. Here's who's taking part: https://zagreb-developers.github.io/drinkup

This year we'll be meeting at Zmajska Pivovara "SIP", Trnjanska cesta 111a (https://maps.app.goo.gl/zHcCyEyGsi5VBwPV9), from 6 PM until midnight.

The format stays the same: no prepared talks, no presentations, no agenda — just drinks, casual conversations and a chance to meet other developers from Zagreb's tech community.

Since several meetup groups are joining forces, this is a great opportunity to step outside your usual community, meet some new people and find out what other developers and groups are up to. And with more people than at our regular drinkups, we're expecting a pretty lively evening!

Feel free to invite your developer friends, colleagues or anyone else from the tech community, even if they're not active members of any of the meetup groups. If you're a developer, you're invited!

See you there! 🍻
